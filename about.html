Our Story

Ten years, four countries, one accountable team.

Kangar started as a small Dubai brokerage helping Indian and Australian families invest in the emirate’s early off-plan wave. Back then, the pitch decks were dazzling but the follow-through was often thin — and we saw first-hand how easily overseas buyers could be talked into the wrong unit, the wrong developer or the wrong ownership structure.

A decade later, we’re a RERA-certified group with offices in Dubai, Melbourne, Auckland and Noida — and the same operating principle: recommendations first, transactions second. Our advisers coordinate with UAE regulators, developers, banks and legal partners every day. When we hand you a shortlist or a business route, it’s the option we’d pick for our own family — and we tell you exactly why.

What We Believe

A few unfashionable ideas.

The best advice sometimes says “not yet.”

Not every enquiry becomes a transaction — and it shouldn’t. If the timing, the unit or the market isn’t right, we’ll say so. A trusted “wait” is worth more than a rushed “yes.”

Speed is not the same as good service.

Fast delivery matters. But rushing a shortlist, a licence route or a bank introduction to hit a made-up deadline is how mistakes happen. We move as quickly as thoroughness allows.

Fees deserve a paper trail.

Every commission, DLD charge, admin fee and third-party cost is itemised in writing — before you sign. If we can’t explain a line item, it doesn’t belong on the invoice.

Kangar Real Estate L.L.C is committed to preventing money laundering, terrorist financing and sanctions breaches across every transaction we facilitate. This policy explains, in plain English, the checks we run before, during and after any client engagement.

Regulatory framework

As a RERA-certified real-estate business operating in Dubai, Kangar complies with UAE Federal Decree Law No. 20 of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing, together with the executive regulations and guidance issued by the Ministry of Economy (MoE), the Central Bank of the UAE and the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU).

Federal Decree Law 20/2018 Ministry of Economy Central Bank UAE FIU-registered entity

KYC procedure

Before executing any transaction, we verify the identity of the client and, where applicable, the ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) of any corporate entity involved. Our KYC process is a four-stage flow, typically completed within one business day.

1
Onboard Client basic details & declaration
2
Identify Government-issued ID & UBO check
3
Screen Sanctions, PEP & adverse-media scan
4
Approve Risk-rated file signed off & stored

Documents we collect

Depending on your residency status and the nature of the transaction, we typically request the following:

Individual clients
  • Passport copy (bio page)
  • Emirates ID (UAE residents)
  • Proof of address (utility / bank)
  • Visa page (UAE residents)
Corporate clients
  • Trade license / Certificate of Incorporation
  • Memorandum & Articles of Association
  • Shareholder register & UBO declaration
  • Board resolution authorising the transaction

Source of funds

For high-value or higher-risk transactions we require documentary evidence of the source of the funds being used. This protects both the client and the wider transaction chain from any downstream regulatory issue.

  • Salary income — 3-6 months of bank statements plus employer confirmation.
  • Business income — audited financials, trade license and business bank statements.
  • Sale of asset — the sale contract and settlement confirmation.
  • Inheritance or gift — probate documents or a notarised gift deed.
  • Investment returns — broker or fund statements evidencing the disposal.
Cash transactions are restricted.

UAE regulation requires cash property transactions above AED 55,000 to be reported. Kangar's policy is to process settlements via regulated banking channels only.

PEP & risk rating

Every onboarded client is risk-rated as Low, Medium or High based on residency, source-of-funds profile, transaction structure and any PEP (Politically Exposed Person) exposure. Higher-risk files receive enhanced due diligence including senior-management sign-off and ongoing monitoring.

A PEP status is not a rejection.

It simply triggers additional documentation and internal approval. We welcome PEP clients who can evidence their source of funds transparently.

Sanctions screening

Every new client and every counterparty in a transaction is screened against the following lists at onboarding and on an ongoing basis:

UAE Local Terrorist List Executive Office for Sanctions
UN Consolidated List UN Security Council
OFAC / EU / UK Lists International sanctions regimes
Zero tolerance.

Any positive match is escalated to our Compliance Officer, the transaction is halted, and a report is filed with the UAE FIU within the mandated timeframe.

Suspicious activity reporting

Our team is trained to identify red flags — unusual transaction patterns, refusal to provide documentation, use of nominee structures or attempts to structure payments below reporting thresholds. Where a red flag is confirmed, we follow the FIU-mandated reporting timeline:

Detect Front-line adviser or automated screening flags the concern to the Compliance Officer.
Investigate Internal review of the file, transaction chain and documentation within 48 hours.
Report Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) filed to the UAE FIU via the goAML portal.
Preserve Client is not tipped off; the file is preserved and monitored per FIU guidance.

Record retention

KYC files, transaction records and supporting evidence are retained on encrypted infrastructure for a minimum of five years from the end of the client relationship, or longer where required by law or an active investigation. Access is restricted to trained staff and audited annually.

Encrypted at rest Role-based access Annually audited

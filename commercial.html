Why We Ask More Questions

Space matched to your licence, activity and visas — not the other way around.

Most commercial searches start with square footage. Ours start with your trade licence. Because the wrong location can block your visa quota, the wrong ceiling height can void your food licence, and the wrong free-zone can restrict your invoicing.

  • Ceiling height and power capacity verified for your activity.
  • Visa quota confirmed against unit size and free-zone rules.
  • Municipality inspection risks flagged before you sign.
  • Ejari and licence-linking done in parallel with lease.

Which space is right for you?

Every type has its own tenancy, licensing and inspection rhythm. Here’s what to look out for.

Office — Grade A / Serviced / Flexi

DIFC, Downtown, Business Bay towers and free-zone flexi-desks. Matched to visa quotas, inspection type and licence category.

Retail — Mall / Street / Kiosk

Mall units, high-street retail and short-lease kiosks. Footfall data and tenant-mix analysis before you commit.

F&B — Licensed & Vent-Ready

Kitchen extraction, food-licence readiness, outdoor seating and alcohol licence considered upfront.

Warehouse & Logistics

Al Quoz, DIP, JAFZA and DAFZA — power, height, loading bays and municipality inspection tier matched to activity.

Showroom & Auto

Medical & Wellness

DHA-approved clinical spaces, wellness centres and pharmacy shells prepared for regulator inspection.

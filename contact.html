Before You Reach Out

Quick answers you might need first.

How fast do you reply?

Within one business day — usually within a few hours during UAE working hours. A real adviser reads and responds, not an auto-responder.

Is my enquiry confidential?

Yes. Enquiries are handled by one adviser and never sold to third parties. Full details in our privacy policy.

Is a consultation free?

Yes — the first 30-minute discovery call is free, whether for property or business setup. Scoping happens before any engagement fee.

Do I need to fly to Dubai?

Not for the first conversation. Video calls, WhatsApp and email work fine. Some steps (bank account opening) usually need one in-person visit — we schedule around your travel.

What languages do you speak?

English, Hindi, Arabic and Urdu across the team. Let us know a preference in the form and we’ll route accordingly.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes — most of our clients are based in Australia, New Zealand, India, the UK or the UAE. Cross-border coordination is standard.

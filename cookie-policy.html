Cookies help our site work properly and let us understand which pages are actually useful. This policy explains, in plain English, what we set, why we set it, and how you can turn it off — without hiding anything in dense legalese.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website you visit. It lets the site remember your actions and preferences (such as language, region or login state) so you don't have to re-enter them on every page. Cookies also help us understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

Cookies set by the site you are visiting are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by a service embedded on that site (like Google Analytics) are called third-party cookies.

Third-party cookies

The only third party that reads cookies on our site is Google Analytics, which gives us aggregated, anonymised traffic data. We do not use Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn Insight, TikTok Pixel or any other advertising tracker.

IP anonymisation is on.

We have configured Google Analytics to anonymise the last octet of every visitor's IP address before it is stored.

Managing cookies

You have three ways to control the cookies we set:

  • On this page — use the preferences card at the top to switch off any non-essential category.
  • In your browser — every modern browser lets you block or clear cookies for specific sites.
  • Via Do Not Track — if your browser sends a DNT signal, we respect it for analytics cookies.

This disclaimer applies to every page, listing, guide, market report and calculator published on kangarrealestate.com. It sits alongside our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy, and is here so you can rely on our content with the right context.

Illustrative pricing

Prices, payment plans and booking terms shown across this website — whether on a project page, buyer guide or comparison table — are indicative. They reflect the best information available at the time of publication.

Final terms depend on live unit availability, the developer's pricing at the time of booking, the applicable payment schedule, and any government or DLD fees that apply on the transfer date.

Pricing can change without notice.

Always confirm the current unit price, service charge and payment schedule with a Kangar adviser before reserving or transferring any funds.

Property listings

Every listing on this site is presented in good faith and refreshed regularly. However, the UAE market moves quickly — a unit shown as available may already be under offer, and specifications, layouts or handover dates may be updated by the developer without prior notice.

Calculator estimates

Any mortgage, ROI, service-charge, DLD-fee or business-setup calculator on this site produces an estimate based on the inputs you supply and current UAE market assumptions. Actual figures depend on your specific circumstances, lender approval, live government fees and the terms agreed at the point of transaction.

Estimates are a starting point.

Use them to compare scenarios — then let a Kangar adviser produce a live, sourced cost sheet for your specific unit or setup.

Regulatory information

UAE regulations covering property ownership, visa eligibility, corporate tax, free-zone activities, banking KYC and cross-border remittance are updated frequently by the relevant federal and emirate-level authorities. While we make every effort to keep our content current, the version you read here may already have been superseded.

Confirm the current rule before you rely on it.

Our advisers cross-check with DLD, DED, GDRFA, FTA and the relevant free-zone authority in real time.

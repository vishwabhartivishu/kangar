About the Project

A new urban signature in Binghatti City.

A new urban community emerges, shaped by the principles of elegance and purity. Defined by precision engineering and a rational, functional grid, Mercedes-Benz Places | Binghatti City introduces an architectural language where every form is intentional and every proportion considered.

Rising 51 storeys with a 4.2m ceiling height, the tower delivers 816 residences over an 8,763 sqm plot — a mix of studios, 1–3 bedroom apartments and 15 ground-floor shops. Curated services span smart-home systems, solar photovoltaic technology, a skyline jogging path, private and podium pools, spa and valet.

Kangar coordinates the full purchase end to end: shortlisting the unit that fits your goal, negotiating with Binghatti, mortgage pre-approval for non-residents, and DLD registration — so you focus on the decision, not the paperwork.

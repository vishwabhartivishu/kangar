Rental FAQs

Common questions from Dubai tenants.

Do I need Ejari?
Yes — Ejari registration is mandatory in Dubai and required for DEWA activation, visa applications and dispute resolution. Kangar handles the registration for you as part of the rental service.
How many cheques for annual rent?
Commonly 1, 2 or 4 cheques — sometimes 6 or 12 depending on the landlord. Fewer cheques usually secure a better price. We negotiate on your behalf.
What’s the security deposit?
Typically 5% of annual rent for unfurnished, 10% for furnished. Fully refundable at end of tenancy subject to property condition — which is why our move-in snagging report matters.
Can the landlord raise the rent at renewal?
Only within RERA rental index caps and after giving 90 days’ written notice. We monitor your renewal date and negotiate on your behalf if an unlawful increase is proposed.
Are pets allowed in Dubai rentals?
Building- and community-specific. We check pet policies upfront so you don’t discover a restriction after moving.
